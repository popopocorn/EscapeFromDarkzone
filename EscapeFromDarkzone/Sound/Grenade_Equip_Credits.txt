Grenade_Equip.wav

Custom edit of Kenney's RPG Audio (Kenney Vleugels).
Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio
License: Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0)
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Source samples:
- Audio/clothBelt.ogg
- Audio/cloth2.ogg
- Audio/handleSmallLeather2.ogg
- Audio/beltHandle1.ogg

Edits: layered pouch/cloth movement, subdued leather/buckle handling,
pitch/time adjustment, EQ, fades, soft transient compression, mono PCM export.
Output: 48 kHz / 16-bit PCM WAV, approximately 0.8 seconds.

Reference research:
OpenSpades Free Assets documents cloth/equipment sounds for grenade actions:
https://github.com/DeathByDenim/openspades-free-pak/blob/main/License/Credits-pak001-Free.md

No commercial game's recording was copied into this asset.
